<p class="eyebrow">Statistics · Research · Teaching</p>

<p class="intro-lead">I study how to uncover the structure of complex data through <em>quantile regression</em> and <em>dimension reduction</em>.</p>

I am a tenure-track **Assistant Professor** in the Department of Statistics at **Western Michigan University**, where I joined in August 2026. My research interests lie in quantile regression, sufficient dimension reduction, and functional data analysis.

I received my Ph.D. in Applied Mathematics (Statistics track) from the **University of North Carolina at Charlotte** in May 2026, advised by **Dr. Eliana Christou**. My dissertation is titled *Dimension Reduction of Conditional Quantiles for Functional Data with Its Extension*.

<div class="research-topics" aria-label="Research interests">
  <span>Quantile regression</span>
  <span>Sufficient dimension reduction</span>
  <span>Functional data analysis</span>
</div>
